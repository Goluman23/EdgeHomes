What moves your number

  • Scope: loose furniture, appliances, curtains and décor are usually extra.
  • Kitchen: shape and counter run (L, U or island) can swing ₹1.5–3 lakh.
  • Wardrobe height: floor-to-ceiling with lofts adds 20–30% to wardrobe cost.
  • Civil changes: breaking walls, re-tiling or re-plumbing adds cost and 1–3 weeks.
  • Material grade: BWP ply, veneer and imported hardware are the biggest jumps.
  • Site: high floors with small lifts, or society work-hour limits, add time.

Where the money goes

  • Foundation and frame: 40–45%
  • Masonry, plaster and façade: 15–20%
  • Plumbing, electrical, HVAC: 15–18%
  • Flooring, doors, windows, finishes: 20–25%
  • Design and approvals: 3–5%

Typical payment plan

Payments are milestone-linked: a booking amount, then stages as work progresses (foundation, each slab, brickwork, services, finishing, handover). You only pay for stages that are done and checked. Your exact schedule is written into your estimate.

Before you compare quotes

  • Is the plywood grade (BWR / BWP / MR) and brand named for every unit?
  • Are hardware brand, hinge type and channel type listed?
  • Are ceilings, electrical, painting and civil work included, or quoted separately later?
  • Is the quote per sq ft of carpet area, super area, or per running ft? Convert before comparing.
  • What is the warranty on workmanship, and who services it after handover?

07 · Possession checklist

Snag check before you sign

Do this walk on the day the builder hands over your flat, before you sign the possession letter. Photograph every snag and get it listed in writing. We do this check free for Edgehomes clients.

Walls, floors, ceilings

  • Hairline cracks, damp patches or bubbling paint
  • Tap tiles with a coin: hollow sound means poor fixing
  • Floor level and slope towards drains in wet areas
  • Ceiling seepage marks under bathrooms above

Doors and windows

  • Doors close without rubbing; locks and stoppers work
  • Window sliders run smoothly; rubber seals intact
  • No light visible around closed window frames

Kitchen

  • Sink drains fast; no leak under the counter
  • Gas pipeline point and chimney cut-out positions
  • Enough sockets for appliances near the counter

Electrical

  • Every switch and socket works (carry a phone charger)
  • MCB / DB labelled; earthing present
  • AC points, geyser points and inverter wiring in place

Plumbing and bathrooms

  • Run every tap and flush for two minutes; check below
  • Hot and cold lines correct; water pressure on top floor
  • Shower area waterproofing; no smell from drains

Paperwork

  • Possession letter, OC status, and final area statement
  • Electricity and water meter readings noted with photos
  • Builder warranties and the list of agreed fixes, signed
  • Floor plan in PDF or DWG for your designer
