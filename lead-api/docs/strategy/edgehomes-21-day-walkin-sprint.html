01The target and the maths

Reverse funnel · 50% buffer

To land 10 walk-ins and 2 signed projects by 15 Nov, plan for 15 walk-ins and 26 booked visits from about 260 leads, at a working budget of ₹1.5 lakh. The 50% buffer covers no-shows, Diwali week and slow closers.

Walk-in

A qualified prospect (owns or is taking possession of a property, budget fits) who meets the team in person at the Edge Homes studio or at a live Edge Homes site, with their floor plan.

Conversion

Signed design agreement plus booking amount received by 15 Nov.

Name the society

Every ad, page and reply names the city and, where possible, the society. "Interiors for BPTP Parklands owners" beats "interiors in Delhi NCR".

Geo-fence tight

Meta ads at 2–3 km around 12–15 named societies per city, not whole cities.

Distance rule

If the studio is 45+ minutes from a pocket, the walk-in for that pocket is a visit to a live Edge Homes site in that city.

  • 60% click-to-WhatsApp, 40% instant forms with 3 questions (possession month, society, BHK)
  • 2–3 km radius around 40–50 named societies, age 28–55, Advantage+ inside the radius

  • 5 angles × 4 cities = 20 ads, 9:16 reels from project footage
  • Kill: >₹800/lead after ₹5,000, or <20% qualified after 15 leads
  • Retargeting ₹8k: site visitors, 50% video viewers, 60-day IG engagers (3–5x cheaper — OwlClaw)

6.2 Google Search · ₹35,000 · 3 walk-ins

  • One campaign per city, exact + phrase only: "interior designer faridabad", "turnkey interiors gurgaon", "3bhk interior cost noida", "house construction palwal", society names + "interior"
  • Negatives: course, job, free, cheap, DIY, software, internship
  • 4 city landing pages, one action, price bands visible
  • ₹1,850/day, 8 AM–10 PM, +20% bids Sat–Sun
  • Google Business Profile (free): 30 photos, weekly Possession Pack post, 10 review requests

6.3 Society activations · ₹30,000 · 3 walk-ins

  • 4 societies with live handovers: 2 Greater Faridabad, 1 Dwarka Expressway, 1 Sector 150 Noida
  • Sat–Sun 31 Oct–1 Nov and 14–15 Nov, 10 AM–6 PM, clubhouse or possession office
  • Kit: 2 standees, 500 flyers with society QR, tablet with that developer's 3D layouts, snag sign-up sheet
  • 40 conversations × 4 society-days = 160 → 48 leads → 19 qualified → 5 booked → 3 walk-ins
  • Society WhatsApp groups: one helpful post (snag checklist), never a sales blast

6.4 Warm database · ₹3,000 · 2 walk-ins

  • 200+ past clients + every enquiry from the last 18 months (target ≥ 500 numbers, with consent)
  • Past clients: founder message — "Anyone in your society taking possession? Free snag check, and ₹25,000 off your next project or a gift voucher for you."
  • Old enquiries: one template with the Possession Pack, a second 5 days later, then stop
  • 500 → 12% reply = 60 → 18 qualified → 4 visits → 2 walk-ins

6.5 Partners · referral fee · 1 walk-in

  • 10 partners: resale/possession brokers, home-loan agents, CAs, structural engineers, Palwal plot dealers
  • 2% of signed contract on first payment (₹30,000 on ₹15 L)
  • Personal wa.me link per partner with their tag

6.6 Instagram organic · ₹0 · 1 walk-in

  • 3 reels/week: before/after, 3D-to-real, client voice — one per city per week
  • Comment keywords "3D", "PRICE", "SNAG" → DM with guide + WhatsApp link
  • Pin the Possession Pack reel; highlights: Faridabad, Gurugram, Noida, Palwal, Process, Prices
